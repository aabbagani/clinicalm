“Catch the problem at step one instead of step five.”

Incoming data does not automatically become trusted data simply because it was successfully ingested. CliniCalm sits at the front of the clinical data pipeline to prevent flawed data from traveling downstream, where diagnosis errors and corrupted cohort selections become exponentially more costly to untangle.

Without intake gate (traditional)

Raw data ingested → unnoticed errors propagate through transformation, cohort curation, and ML models → corrupted analyses discovered weeks later, at step 5.

With CliniCalm gate (at intake)

Raw data ingested → deterministic schema, domain, and consistency rules run instantly → flawed records quarantined at step 1, with human-in-the-loop review.
